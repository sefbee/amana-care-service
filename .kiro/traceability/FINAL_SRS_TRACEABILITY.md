## Approved identity decision

The user has explicitly decided to distinguish the Platform Superuser (PSU) from the SRS System Administrator (SA). PSU is unrestricted for technical/business platform actions, with explicit audit for all sensitive reads, reveals, overrides and break-glass actions. SA remains subject to SRS restrictions on PII, sensitive vault documents and safeguarding cases. PSU must not be modeled as an SA permission shortcut.
